## 5. Architectural observation — B4's trust-shift (CMP-02, the critic's sharpest point)

B4 makes the **stored row's `event_code == ORDER_VERIFIED`** the sole revenue authority. Recording revenue on a
genuinely OV-labelled row is exactly what RULE-003/FAIL-001 *permit* — so B4 correctly closes the quote/draft-as-revenue
hole. But B4 does not (and is not meant to) validate that the OV **label itself** is genuine. The defense against a
self-minted OV label is **endpoint authN/authZ (M6-OD-011, a standing blocker) + Commerce-ownership of verification**,
not B4. This is armed-not-fired today because the materialize/CTR-023 worker is **un-wired** (no `app/api/*` endpoint
calls it — confirmed by grep across prior passes) and M6-OD-011 is OPEN. Stated plainly so the owner sees where the
revenue-trust burden now sits.
